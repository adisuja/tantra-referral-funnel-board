PROCESS.md

Outreach process: import, personalise, send

How each team member gets every personal contact into the Tantra referral funnel and invites them from their own accounts. Phase 1 runs today on what Tantra already has. Phase 2 (the gaps at the end) makes it one screen in Tantra.

1. Import every contact (about 15 minutes per person)

There is no one-screen import in Tantra yet (gap G12). Each source has a quick official export, and one command merges them all.

Source How to export it What it gives
Google Contacts and Gmail https://contacts.google.com > Export > Contacts (all) and Other contacts > Google CSV. "Other contacts" is everyone you have emailed Names, emails, phones, company, title
Phone contacts (iPhone) https://www.icloud.com/contacts > select all > Export vCard Names, mobiles, emails, company
Phone contacts (Android) Contacts app > Fix and manage > Export to file (.vcf) Same
WhatsApp and SMS The same people as the phone contacts: WhatsApp reads your phone's address book, so the vCard covers them Mobiles for WhatsApp and SMS
LinkedIn https://www.linkedin.com/mypreferences/d/download-my-data > Connections > Request archive (ready in about 10 minutes) Name, company, position, profile link

Then one command merges and de-duplicates everything (one person, one row, matched on email, phone or LinkedIn):

python3 tools/import_contacts.py adi adi-contacts.csv contacts.csv phone.vcf Connections.csv

For each person it picks the channel to reach them on (WhatsApp if they have a mobile, then LinkedIn, then email) and writes the personal lines from facts in the export only (their company and title). Nothing is invented.

2. Personal page for every prospect

python3 tools/push_prospects.py adi-contacts.csv adi-links.csv

Every person gets their own page, https://hello.jointantra.com/adi/?r=TOKEN, which opens with their name, their company and their personal line above the fold, and the button "Sign me up for the trial already, Adi". Link previews in WhatsApp and LinkedIn are personal too. Tokens never change on re-runs.

3. Review, then load into Tantra

  1. The sender reads adi-links.csv and deletes anyone they would not message personally. This is the approval of the batch.
  2. The rows load into the pipeline Tantra referral funnel, stage Invited to try, with custom fields channel, link, why and sender (loaded by our build session through Tantra's deal import until gap G12 gives the team an Import button).
  3. Entering the stage starts that sender's sequence.

4. The automation (one sequence per team member)

Live in Tantra: Tantra referral funnel: Adi (stage Invited to try). Each sender gets a copy pinned to their own accounts until gap G13 lets one sequence send from each deal owner's accounts.

Day Step Channel and account
0 Message 1 (OUTREACH.md) WhatsApp, LinkedIn or email, whichever channel says, from the sender's own account
2 SMS with the page link and STOP line Tantra's numbers; Tantra applies country rules (US and Canada need consent; India and the UK only as a WhatsApp fallback)
3 Nudge Same channel as message 1
7 Last nudge with the setup call link Same channel

Every step stops the moment the person replies, and sends only 10:00 to 19:00 India time, Monday to Saturday. Daily caps: WhatsApp 50, LinkedIn 60 messages per account (Tantra's limits).
