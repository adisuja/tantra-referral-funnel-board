Plan: the Tantra referral funnel as a funnel type in Tantra, run from the chat

Status: plan only, nothing built. Decisions answered by Adi on 23 Nov 2026 (section 8). Code facts are from adisuja/tantra-crm origin/main 30eda7c0; B = tantra-backend-v2/crm-worker/src, F = tantra-frontend-v2/crm-app/src.

1. Goal and done means

It is a product feature (Adi, 23 Nov): any customer workspace can say "set up a referral funnel" in the chat and launch its own referral funnel for its own product: every team member's contacts imported, a personal page per prospect, invites going out from each member's own WhatsApp, LinkedIn and email plus SMS, the workspace's own offer (a trial, a call or a checkout), the call booked, and the numbers reported back in chat. Tantra's own launch is the first workspace to run it, with the Tantra Pro trial that turns on both apps.

Done when, on the QA workspace with test contacts only: 1. One chat conversation creates the funnel, asks only what it cannot infer, and shows a plan with progress. 2. Two test members import contacts from Google, a vCard and a LinkedIn export through one screen; duplicates merge; first owner wins. 3. Each test prospect's page shows their name, company and personal line; the button names their referrer. 4. Each member approves their first batch from a chat card; messages leave from that member's own accounts; nothing sends without the approval. 5. A test prospect clicks, starts the trial (Dodo test), books the setup call, and their deal moves Invited to try, Watched, Trial started, Call booked, owned by the referrer. 6. The chat answers "how is the referral funnel doing?" with per member numbers.

2. What it looks like in the chat

The owner (Adi) 1. "Set up a referral funnel." The chat calls crm_funnel_create with kind referral; the build plan appears with its steps. 2. The chat fills the offer from the workspace's own funnel settings (settings.offer, B/funnelOs/offerModel.ts: trial days, price, button link) and asks for confirmation on one card. For Tantra's own workspace that is Tantra Pro, $99 a month after 7 days, with $30 of credits. 3. Every team member can import and send (Adi, 23 Nov). The chat lists them and shows who has WhatsApp, LinkedIn and email connected; a card goes to each member who is missing an account, with the connect link. 4. "Upload your video or paste its link." The page preview updates. 5. It shows three sample personal pages built from real imported contacts and the message 1 draft per channel; the owner edits or approves. 6. It runs a dry run on test contacts and shows the log. 7. Launch stays the owner's button (crm_funnel_launch_all and go_live are blocked in chat today and stay blocked).

Each team member 1. A chat card: "Import your contacts" opens the one import screen. 2. After import: "412 people, 38 skipped (no name). Review them?" They untick anyone they would not message. 3. Every morning: "Today's 40 invites are ready: approve, edit or skip." One click sends the batch inside their daily caps. 4. Replies come back into their chat as "Priya replied: draft an answer?".

Anyone: "How is the referral funnel doing?" returns invited, replied, watched, trial, booked, paying, per member and per channel.

3.2 Template: pages, booking, pipeline, sequences

New B/sites/templates/referral.ts (a TemplateDef): - Pages: invite (per referrer and per prospect), welcome (after the offer, with active, failed and existing states), booked. Port the live hello.jointantra.com pages; every word that names Tantra, the product or the offer comes from the workspace's brand and offer settings, so a customer's funnel speaks about their product. A page per member is generated from the team list, so new members get a page automatically. - Domain: each workspace serves its funnel on its own domain (crm_pages_add_domain) or the Tantra pages default. Tantra's own funnel stays on hello.jointantra.com (Adi, 23 Nov), so links already sent keep working. - Booking: "Setup call", 20 minutes, group calendar, lands in Call booked. - Pipeline: Invited to try, Replied, Watched, Trial started, Call booked, Call done, Paying, Not now, Cancelled. - Sequences: message 1, SMS day 2, nudges day 3 and 7 (OUTREACH.md), and trial sequences A to D (TRIAL_SEQUENCES.md), using the new owner routing below.

3.3 Personal pages per visitor (new capability)

Today pages fill {{token}} placeholders from workspace and funnel settings only, and the page cache keys on host and path (B/sites/pageCache.ts:5-14). - Add prospect slots to the page renderer: {{prospect.first_name}}, {{prospect.company}}, {{prospect.hook}}, {{referrer.name}}, each with a fallback. - When a request carries a recipient token (trk_recipient_tokens, the existing per contact token), render uncached on the server: token to contact to deal, fill the slots, set a first-party cookie so the welcome and booked pages stay personal, add per prospect link preview tags. - Same design as the live Worker (hello.jointantra.com, worker/src/index.js), moved inside Tantra so the data comes from the deal and no separate store is needed.

3.4 One import screen per member (gap G12)

New B/contacts/ module and an F page "Import my contacts": - Google Contacts and Other contacts via the People API (add contacts.readonly and contacts.other.readonly to GOOGLE_SCOPES in B/google.ts:11). - Gmail correspondents and WhatsApp chats with full pagination (B/referralFunnel/outreach.ts:41-68 read one page of 100 today). - LinkedIn connections via Unipile relations. - vCard and CSV upload (the merge logic of tools/import_contacts.py, ported). - Merge on email, E.164 phone and LinkedIn URL; contact owner per member with first owner wins (new owner_user_id on contacts, gap G17). - Each imported person becomes a deal in Invited to try, owned by the member, with custom.channel chosen (WhatsApp, then LinkedIn, then email).

3.5 Personal line per prospect (gap G15)

An AI task referral.hook beside referral.compose (B/referralFunnel/ai.ts): from company, title and, where available, a recent LinkedIn post or company news found by enrichment, write one sentence in the sender's voice, store it with its source in custom.hook and custom.hook_source. No source, no line (the page falls back).

3.6 Sending from each member's own accounts (gap G13)

  • New step sender owner in B/routes/automation.ts (:950, :990, :1007, :1109) and B/senders.ts:270: resolve the deal owner's own WhatsApp, LinkedIn or mailbox by connected_by (the logic of pickAccount, B/referralFunnel/connect.ts:74), skip with a reason if they have none.
  • One sequence serves the whole team; no copy per member.
  • SMS number per member, falling back to the workspace route (gap G18).
  • Daily caps per member (Adi, 23 Nov): WhatsApp 50, LinkedIn 60, email 30, plus Tantra's existing per account rate limits and spacing between sends. A batch never releases more than what is left of the day's cap.

3.7 Approval before anything sends (gap G14)

  • New step option approval: "batch": runs due today wait in a per owner queue instead of sending.
  • Each morning (and on demand) the owner gets a chat card with the batch: approve all, edit one, skip one. Approval releases exactly what was shown, inside caps (the referral funnel's approved send rule, B/referralFunnel/outreach.ts).
  • Uses the existing team card seam crm_team_card / crm_team_card_decide (B/routes/teamTools.ts:20-22).

3.8 Tracking and stages (gap G16)

  • {{tracked_link}} merge tag: the member's invite page with the prospect's recipient token.
  • Page events from t.js (visit, video 25 to 100 percent, trial click) move the deal to Watched; conditions link.clicked and page.watched for steps.
  • Trial and payment from the activation (below) move Trial started and Paying; bookings move Call booked; replies move Replied (existing stop on reply).

3.9 The offer step: any workspace's own, Tantra's single activation

A customer workspace's invite page uses its own offer from settings.offer: a trial or checkout link through its own payment connection (crm_trk_save_payment_connection, so trial started and paid move the deal), or a call only. Tantra's own workspace uses the single activation (PRD 9a, gaps G2 to G11): one Dodo checkout for Tantra Pro, $99 a month after a 7 day trial, $30 of usage credits, Dodo's trial as the only clock, and the main app reading Tantra's entitlement (main app request G5, G6). That is a dependency of Tantra's own funnel going public, not of the feature.

8. Decisions (Adi, 23 Nov 2026)

  1. Every team member can import and send. No per member switch; the owner sees everyone's numbers.
  2. Daily caps per member: WhatsApp 50, LinkedIn 60, email 30, inside Tantra's rate limits.
  3. A product feature for every workspace. Any client, customer or user of the Tantra app can launch their own referral funnel in any workspace; nothing is Tantra specific in the kind, the template or the chat.
  4. Tantra's pages stay on hello.jointantra.com after the move into Tantra.

What decision 3 adds to the build

  • Copy, brand, offer and domain read from each workspace's settings (3.2, 3.9); no Tantra wording in the template.
  • Billing: imports, AI lines and sends use the workspace's own credits and channel costs, like every other funnel, with the $30 trial credits covering a new Tantra user's first runs.
  • Limits for every workspace: the same caps, opt outs, STOP handling, consent rules per country and test workspace rules apply to customers' funnels, not only ours.
  • The gallery card, help text and the chat's guided build speak to a customer launching their own product (Batch 8).
