Tantra referral funnel: outreach messages

Each team member sends these to people they already know. The first message comes from the brief Adi wrote, tightened, with the broken personalisation clause fixed. Every message links to the sender's own page, so each lead is credited to the person who invited them.

Fields

Field Where it comes from If it is missing
{first_name} Contact record Skip the contact (no "Hey there")
{company} Enrichment: current employer on LinkedIn or the email domain Use the version without a company
{focus} AI field: one short fact about their work right now, from their headline, a recent post or company news, under 8 words, lower case, e.g. "scaling the outbound team" Use the version without a focus
{link} The sender's tracked page link, with a recipient token, e.g. https://hello.jointantra.com/adi/?r=TOKEN Never send without it
{sender_first_name} The sending team member Required

The AI only writes {focus} from a fact it can quote from a source, and it saves that source on the contact. No fact, no clause. Every batch is read and approved in Tantra before it goes out.

Message 1: first touch (day 0)

LinkedIn and WhatsApp

Hey {first_name}, been a while since we had a chance to talk.

We built something new: an omni-channel AI RevOps engine that automates the whole revenue process, from finding leads to booking calls. I noticed you're at {company} working on {focus}, so I thought it would be relevant.

I'd love for you to take a look and send me your feedback. Would you be interested?

Here's a short video of what it does: {link}

Without {focus}: "I noticed you're at {company}, so I thought it would be relevant." Without {company} or {focus}: "Given what you work on, I thought it would be relevant."

Email

Subject: {first_name}, quick favour?

Hey {first_name},

Been a while since we had a chance to talk.

We built something new: an omni-channel AI RevOps engine that automates the whole revenue process, from finding leads to booking calls. I noticed you're at {company} working on {focus}, so I thought it would be relevant.

I'd love for you to take a look and send me your feedback. Here's a short video of what it does, and you can try it free for 7 days from the same page:

{link}

Would you be interested?

{sender_first_name}

Message 2: if no reply (day 3, same channel)

Hey {first_name}, just bumping this in case it got buried. The video is under 5 minutes: {link}. Even a one line reaction would help us a lot.

Message 3: if no reply (day 7, same channel, last one)

Last nudge from me, {first_name}. If now's not the time, no problem at all. If you'd rather just talk it through, grab 20 minutes here: {booking_link}

Messages 2 and 3 go into the sender's daily approval queue like message 1: one click sends the day's batch. Nothing else is sent after message 3. A reply, a page visit that starts the trial, a booked call or an opt out stops the sequence at once.

When they reply

Reply What Tantra does Who answers
"Yes, send it" or a question Moves the deal to Replied, drafts an answer with the link and the booking link The sender approves the draft
Watched, no trial yet Moves the deal to Watched, sends message 2 early with the trial line Automatic after approval
"Not now" or "no" Closes the deal as Not now, stops everything, sets a 90 day reminder Nobody
"Stop" or "unsubscribe" Suppresses the contact on every channel Nobody

Sending rules

  • Personal contacts only: first degree LinkedIn connections, saved phone contacts and people the sender has emailed before. Never the cold calendar list or any bought list.
  • Email goes from the sender's own mailbox (their personal or jointantra.com address), never a cold sending domain, at most 30 a day.
  • LinkedIn: at most 25 first messages a day per person. WhatsApp: at most 20 a day per person, 1 to 1, never a broadcast list.
  • A contact gets the funnel from one team member only. If two team members both know someone, the first to add them owns the contact.
